Biography

About NaldoA

NaldoA is a Brazilian artist passionate about music and technology, blending both worlds to create the sound of the future.

As a DJ and producer, he moves between electronic music, EDM and reggaeton, building a sonic identity where futuristic synths meet Latin rhythms.

Founder of NaldoA Digital Music (2024), his mission is to release music independently, connect cultures through sound and push sound design forward.
